# 5 · GTM & Consulting Playbook

*How you, as the data consultant, take this to group companies and build a practice around it.*

## 5.1 The business you are building

Two engines on one chassis:

1. **Services engine (cash now):** diagnostic → pilot → rollout → managed service. Classic consulting, but productised: fixed scopes, fixed prices, fast cycles because the platform does the heavy lifting.
2. **Product engine (compounding):** subscriptions per group + per entity, plus the growing industry mapping knowledge base that reduces your delivery cost every quarter.

Target split by year 3: ~40% product / 60% services revenue, with services margin rising from ~45% to ~65% as the knowledge base compounds.

## 5.3 The demo script (built around this prototype)

The prototype is your best salesperson. 30-minute flow:

1. **(3 min) The pain mirror.** Open the group command center. "This is every entity, department and currency on one surface — drill anywhere."
2. **(10 min) The magic moment.** Go to **AI Ingest Studio** → "Run pipeline" on *Regional sales export* (or better: a file **they** sent you beforehand). Watch it: header detected under the title row, types inferred, currency symbols stripped, duplicates and TOTAL rows dropped, `Sales Rep` excluded, columns mapped with confidence bars.
3. **(5 min) The trust moment.** Override one mapping in front of them ("the AI proposes, you decide"), then **Commit** — show the patch report: rows added, boards lit, coercions logged. Refresh the command center; their numbers moved.
4. **(5 min) The warehouse.** Show `/warehouse`: KPI dictionary, lineage chain, source registry with quality scores. "Every number on every board traces back to the file it came from."
5. **(5 min) Ask AI.** Type "which entity has the best margin?" — real numbers, governed data.
6. **(2 min) The close.** "Two weeks to tell you exactly what this looks like for your group." Book the Diagnostic.

**Rules:** always demo with *their* data if possible (Tier-1/2 files only — pre-screen); never claim full automation; show the human gate proudly.

## 5.4 Sales narrative per persona

- **CFO/Chairman:** consolidation speed, comparability, one truth. ROI line: "N analyst-days per month × M entities, today."
- **Entity FD:** "stop reformatting" — the emotional hook that gets internal sponsors.
- **IT/Security:** tenancy, RLS, audit, regional hosting. Hand them §3.5 of the architecture doc.
- **The sceptical analyst:** show the review console — the AI is their assistant, not their replacement.

## 5.5 Land & expand within groups

Groups are wired for land-and-expand: the chairman owns many companies, and boardrooms talk.

- **Land** on the entity with the most reporting pain (usually the one the CFO trusts least).
- **Expand** at the quarterly review, when the chairman sees the first consolidated board: "we can onboard the next entity in two weeks."
- **Deepen** department by department (finance → sales → operations → supply chain), each a small yes.
- **Gate the ritual:** the monthly close becomes "drop files → review mappings → boards update." Once the ritual is in the calendar, churn requires breaking a habit — which nobody does.

Reference-ability: one credible group logo in a business community (GCC family groups, industry associations, chambers) is worth more than any marketing spend.

## 5.6 Delivery methodology (per engagement)

1. **Frame (wk 1):** reporting inventory (who sends what to whom, in which format), KPI workshop (name → formula → owner — the KPI dictionary is born), success criteria.
2. **Onboard (wk 2–4):** ingest historical files entity by entity; run the review console *with* the client's analyst seated next to you (knowledge transfer by construction); ratify mappings.
3. **Ratify (wk 5):** governance council (CFO + entity FDs + you) signs the KPI dictionary and goes live.
4. **Operate (ongoing):** managed-service rhythm — monthly review, drift alerts, new sources, board pack.

**Golden rule:** the client's analyst must click the final *Commit* themselves in the first month. Ownership of the gate transfers; trust transfers with it.

## 5.7 Partner & scale path

- **Year 1:** you are the product's only delivery arm — deliberately, to keep feedback tight.
- **Year 2:** certify 2–4 associate consultants on the methodology; the review console and runbooks make them productive in weeks.
- **Year 3:** channel partnerships with accounting firms and ERP resellers who already sit in group CFO conversations; you become the platform + certification body.
