# Meridian AI — One-Pager

### Send us your spreadsheets. We'll send you the truth.

---

**The problem.** Group leadership runs on numbers that are weeks old, manually assembled from a hundred Excel files, impossible to drill into, and different in every entity. Enterprise data tools assume teams that group companies don't have.

**The product.** An AI-powered group data platform:

- **Any file in.** Drop any spreadsheet — no template, no naming rules. The AI detects the structure, profiles every column, writes a cleaning recipe, and maps the file onto the group's data model.
- **One governed warehouse.** Every entity, department, currency and period lands in one canonical model — with full lineage from any board number back to the source file.
- **Leadership boards out.** Group command center, 150+ department boards, drill-downs, natural-language Q&A, board-pack exports.
- **Humans approve.** The AI proposes mappings with confidence scores; your people confirm in minutes. Nothing reaches the board unaudited.

**Why it's different.** Alteryx-class platforms are built for analysts at enterprises with $100K+ analytics budgets. We are built for **groups of companies**: entity-native, department-native, consolidation-native — and delivered by consultants who run the monthly cycle with you, not by a licence that sits on a shelf.

**How it lands.**

| Step | Time | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnostic | 2 weeks | Reporting reality mapped; business case for the board |
| Pilot (one entity) | 6–8 weeks | Live boards on your real data |
| Group rollout | 1–3 weeks per entity | Consolidated command center |
| Managed service | ongoing | The monthly close, run for you |

**The proof.** A working prototype ingests genuinely messy files — mixed date formats, currency symbols in numbers, duplicate rows, totals rows, seven spellings of four channels — and turns them into governed boards, with every AI decision inspectable and overridable.
